METHODMESH v15.2 NON-DATA STATE
=============================
ODK Central is the authoritative research-data store.
This directory must contain no submission XML, photos, attachments,
CSV/XLS exports, or raw submission-answer JSON.
Persistent contents are restricted to UUIDs, timestamps, field names,
comments/reasons, cryptographic hashes, verification states, service
metadata, public TSA certificates and cryptographic checkpoints.
